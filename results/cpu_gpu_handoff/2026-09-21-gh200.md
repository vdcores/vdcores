# GH200 CPU/GPU Handoff Results

Date: 2026-09-21  
Instance: `anant_testing`  
Tested commit: `a64a1093c6485051629497c257f0d4c0bd38e56f`

## Environment

- NVIDIA GH200 480GB, 97,871 MiB reported GPU memory
- NVIDIA driver 580.105.08
- CUDA toolkit 12.8.93
- PyTorch 2.7.0 with CUDA 12.8
- Python 3.10.12
- Linux 6.8.0-1046-nvidia-64k, ARM64

CUDA reported all handoff capabilities as available:

- pageable memory access
- host-page-table access
- host-native atomics
- concurrent managed access

## Method

Each trial used 500 warmup iterations followed by 10,000 measured iterations.
The system-memory trials used an ordinary CPU-resident `torch.int32` tensor and
required host-page-table hardware coherence. Pinned trials used CUDA page-locked
host memory. The benchmark measured notification from the first GPU atomic to
CPU observation and the complete GPU-to-CPU-to-GPU counter round trip.

```bash
python3 app/python/cpu_gpu_handoff.py \
  --require-hardware-coherence --warmup 500 --iterations 10000 --json

python3 app/python/cpu_gpu_handoff.py \
  --pinned --warmup 500 --iterations 10000 --json
```

## Results

| Allocation | Trial | GPU to CPU median | GPU to CPU p95 | Round-trip median | Round-trip p95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| System | 1 | 536.325 us | 629.605 us | 539.589 us | 632.358 us |
| System | 2 | 535.909 us | 624.070 us | 539.525 us | 627.365 us |
| System | 3 | 535.941 us | 576.998 us | 539.364 us | 579.814 us |
| System | 4 | 534.853 us | 556.389 us | 538.405 us | 560.293 us |
| System | 5 | 911.848 us | 1,062.057 us | 1,049.322 us | 1,199.915 us |
| System | 6 | 536.709 us | 592.550 us | 540.101 us | 595.238 us |
| Pinned | 1 | 539.653 us | 626.917 us | 542.757 us | 629.797 us |
| Pinned | 2 | 534.053 us | 558.629 us | 537.445 us | 562.182 us |
| Pinned | 3 | 538.149 us | 582.502 us | 541.445 us | 585.318 us |
| Pinned | 4 | 536.165 us | 573.222 us | 539.685 us | 577.062 us |

The median of the six system-trial medians was 536.134 us GPU-to-CPU and
539.557 us round trip. Five system trials formed a tight cluster; trial 5 was a
clear transient outlier, with a 22.4 ms maximum. The median of the four pinned
trial medians was 537.157 us GPU-to-CPU and 540.565 us round trip.

At this benchmark granularity, pinned memory did not improve the typical
latency over an ordinary coherent CPU tensor. In the stable system trials, the
CPU acknowledgement and GPU observation added roughly 3.4 us to the median
GPU-to-CPU notification time.

These measurements include Python/PyBind dispatch and fresh CUDA kernel launch
overhead on every iteration. They validate the coherent atomic protocol but do
not represent the expected cost of an in-runtime persistent-kernel handoff.

The complete raw measurements are in `2026-09-21-gh200.json`.

## Build Notes

The Lambda image's distro PyTorch package did not include PyBind11 headers.
The standalone extension built after installing `pybind11` in the user
environment and passing its include directory through `CPATH`:

```bash
python3 -m pip install --user pybind11
CPATH="$(python3 -c 'import pybind11; print(pybind11.get_include())')" make handoff
```
